Artisane Daily Publisher

Privacy policy

This personal tool is operated by the owner of the Artisane Pinterest account, @artisane131. It is intended only to publish that owner's prepared artwork and is not offered for other users to sign up.

Data the app processes

  • Pinterest account username and board identifiers used to verify the publishing destination.
  • The owner's selected images, Pin titles, descriptions, alt text and daily publishing schedule.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, and the app credentials needed to authenticate API requests. The app does not request the Pinterest account password.
  • Publishing results, including Pin IDs, URLs, timestamps and status records used to prevent duplicate posts and investigate failures.

The app reads the authenticated owner's own Pins and Boards. It does not request general access to other Pinners' private data, messages or contacts.

Why data is used

Data is used to authenticate the owner's account, choose the destination board, publish the prepared content, renew API authorization when permitted, and record whether a scheduled post succeeded.

Storage and retention

The prepared implementation stores the queue, publishing history and app credentials on the operator's computer. The credentials file is restricted to the local user and excluded from version control. Credentials are not intentionally included in application logs or shown on this website.

Queue and history records remain until the operator removes them. OAuth credentials remain until the operator removes them, they expire, or access is revoked. Pinterest retains published Pins according to its own policies; removing a local queue entry does not delete a published Pin.

Data sent to services

Authentication requests, selected Pin content and destination identifiers are sent to Pinterest through its official API. This personal app has no advertising, data-sale or marketing-data distribution feature.

These static website pages do not contain analytics scripts, advertising trackers, sign-up forms or app token storage. A hosting provider may process routine web-request information to deliver the pages. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process web-request information under its own privacy statement.

Control, revocation and deletion

The account owner can stop the scheduled task, revoke the app's Pinterest access in their account settings, and delete the local credentials and publishing files. Published Pins can be managed separately on Pinterest. Requests or questions can be sent to ainguyen.forwork@gmail.com or directed to the owner through the Artisane profile.

Changes

This policy describes the local, single-account implementation. This policy will be reviewed before use with a server, external storage, additional analytics, other users or a different data-sharing arrangement.
